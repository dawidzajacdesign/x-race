Personal data protection

1. What is the GDPR?

GDPR is the commonly used abbreviation for the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC, OJ L 119, p. 1), known in Polish as “RODO” (hereinafter: GDPR).

2. What is the purpose of the GDPR?

The main purpose of the regulation is to improve the protection of the personal data of natural persons and to harmonise the rules for processing personal data across the European Union.

3. Since when has the GDPR applied?

The GDPR has applied since 25 May 2018.

7. On what basis and for how long does HART process your personal data?

We process personal data for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

  1. to conclude and perform a contract, including fulfilling orders and ensuring the proper quality of services – for the term of the contract (including fulfilment of the order) and settlements after it ends, under Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (hereinafter this legal basis is referred to as: performance of a contract);
  2. to comply with our legal obligations, under Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (hereinafter this legal basis is referred to as: legal obligation), e.g.:
    • issuing and storing invoices and other accounting documents,
    • handling complaints and responding to them,
    which we will use for the following period:
    • for the period required by law for storing data, e.g. tax data,
    • for the time needed to perform the obligations, e.g. issuing invoices, or for the period during which, under the law, we may bear legal consequences of failing to meet statutory obligations, e.g. receive a fine from state or local authorities, i.e. under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (hereinafter this legal basis is referred to as: our legitimate interest);
  3. for direct marketing – including sending offers and commercial and promotional information electronically (legal basis: our legitimate interest), for the term of the contract, until you withdraw your consent or object to the processing of your data for this purpose;
  4. to pursue and defend against claims – for the term of the contract (legal basis: performance of a contract), until the limitation period for claims arising from our contracts expires, and if we pursue claims – for the duration of the relevant proceedings (legal basis: our legitimate interest).

8. What rights do you have under the GDPR?

In connection with the processing of your personal data by HART, you have the following rights:

  1. access to your personal data,
  2. erasure of your personal data (right to be forgotten),
  3. updating/rectification of your personal data,
  4. portability of your personal data,
  5. restriction of processing of your personal data.

You may also object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing. Once you object, we will be obliged to stop processing your data for this purpose.

In particularly justified situations you may also object at any time to our processing of your personal data where the legal basis for its use is our legitimate interest or the public interest. We will then no longer be entitled to process the personal data covered by the objection on that basis, unless we demonstrate grounds for establishing, pursuing or defending claims, or compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which, under the law, override your interests, rights and freedoms.

The scope of these rights and the cases in which you can exercise them follow from the law and depend, among other things, on the legal basis on which HART uses the data.

To exercise the above rights, please contact us at rodo@hartphp.com.pl. Erasure, portability, restriction of and objection to processing may affect the services we provide and may make it impossible to perform them properly.

9. Do you have to consent to HART processing your data?

We are entitled to process certain categories of data without your additional consent; however, to process your data for purposes other than those provided for by law, we need your consent. Giving consent is not mandatory, and once given it can be withdrawn at any time (this does not affect the lawfulness of the use of your data before the consent was withdrawn).

To exercise the above rights, please contact us at rodo@hartphp.com.pl.

10. Does HART transfer your data outside the EEA?

We do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area (the European Union plus Norway, Liechtenstein and Iceland). If, during our cooperation, it becomes necessary to transfer such data, we will do so to the extent permitted by law.

11. Can you lodge a complaint about the processing of your personal data?

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, Poland) if you believe that the processing of your personal data by HART infringes the law.
