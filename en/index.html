FAQ

X-Race discs and pads: questions and answers.

What is the difference between drilled and slotted discs?

Drilled holes carry heat and water away faster, while slots clean dust and gases off the pad surface. X-Race discs combine both, so braking stays stable even at high temperatures.

Are X-Race discs suitable for everyday driving?

Yes. They are designed for heavy loads, but thanks to 360° corrosion protection and a pad compound that reduces dust and noise, they work just as well in everyday use.

What is brake fade?

Brake fade is a drop in braking performance after several hard stops, when the pads and discs overheat. X-Race pads, made from a low metallic, carbon and ceramic compound, are fade-resistant.

Do the discs need degreasing before fitting?

No. X-Race discs are ready to fit straight out of the box.

What is the High Carbon version?

These are discs cast from iron with a higher carbon content, which damp vibration better and shed heat faster.

Where can I buy X-Race discs and pads?

From shops and workshops working with the HART network in Poland, the Czech Republic and Slovakia. You will find the nearest location on the HART branch map.
