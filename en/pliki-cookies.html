Cookies on the X-Race website

On your first visit to x-race.hartphp.pl we ask for your consent to cookies. Until you choose, we run only what is essential for the website to work.

Essential

They keep the website working and remember your cookie choice (stored in the browser for 12 months). They are always on.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4, activated via Google Tag Manager only after you consent. It lets us see how you use the website and improve it.

Marketing

Google Ads, activated only after you consent. Used to target X-Race ads and measure their effectiveness.

YouTube

The video on the website loads from YouTube only after you click the play button. Before that, YouTube does not store cookies on your device.

You can change your consent at any time.

HART cookie rules

  1. Hart Sp. z o.o. uses cookies. Cookies are small text files sent to web browsers. They are stored on the devices of users of www.hartphp.com.pl and store.hartphp.com.pl and are sent back to these websites on subsequent visits.
  2. Cookies are collected solely to gather information in order to tailor sales to particular products and to Customers' individual needs and preferences. Cookies are stored by the web browser on the Customer's hard drive and are not directly used to collect personal data. This means the Customer can disable cookies in their web browser, but this will limit or completely prevent the use of www.hartphp.com.pl and store.hartphp.com.pl.
  3. Hart Sp. z o.o. collects IP addresses during internet connections for technical purposes related to server administration. They are used for statistics and to manage cookies better.
  4. Hart Sp. z o.o. also informs Customers that the web browser can be configured to prevent cookies from being stored on the Customer's device.
  5. Hart Sp. z o.o. also points out that the Customer can delete cookies after they have been stored by the Seller, using the relevant browser functions, dedicated programs or the tools available in the Customer's operating system.
  6. The links below explain how to delete cookies in the most popular web browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Opera, Internet Explorer.
  7. Hart Sp. z o.o. also informs Customers that changing the browser configuration to prevent or limit the storage of cookies on the Customer's device may restrict the functionality of the services provided. Deleting cookies while a service is being provided may have similar effects.
  8. Hart Sp. z o.o. informs Customers that it uses Google Analytics, which collects online identifiers including cookies. More information is available in the privacy policy (section 14, Google Analytics).
